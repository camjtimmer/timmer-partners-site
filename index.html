Four ways to structure a deal.

Some owners want a clean exit. Some want to stay in the deal and keep their upside. Plenty just need better debt. Tell us what you're after; we'll tell you which structure fits.

01

Direct Acquisition

The straightforward version: we buy your building outright. For owners ready to monetize and move on, or investors looking to trade out.

02

Sale-Leaseback

Sell your building and stay right where you are as the tenant. Your equity becomes working capital, and your operation never moves.

03

Partnership Sale-Leaseback

Pull out up to 100% of your building's value while keeping ownership in the deal. We buy the real estate, you keep an equity stake, and we share the upside.

04

Debt Solutions

We secure and personally guarantee financing on your behalf, giving you access to better terms than you'd get on your own.
